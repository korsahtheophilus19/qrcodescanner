Frequently Q&A

Do I need to download or install an app to scan a QR code?

No download required. This is a fully web-based utility app that loads instantly in any modern mobile or desktop browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge). You can scan barcodes and QR codes immediately without clogging up your phone's storage.

Is this web QR scanner private and secure?

Yes, 100%. Our scanner features secure, zero-server architecture. All image processing and QR decoding happen locally directly inside your device's browser canvas framework using JavaScript. No camera stream, photos, or data are ever uploaded, transmitted, or saved to an external database.

How do I scan a QR code from a screenshot or photo gallery image?

Simply click the image upload or browse area, select the screenshot or photo from your local file directory, and our engine will read the pixel data using an off-screen canvas to decode the code automatically. There is no need to use a separate phone camera to scan a screen.

Why is my camera not working or opening?

Browser-based camera utilities require explicit permissions to activate. If your stream is blocked, click the Lock Icon 🔒 next to the URL address bar in your browser, change the Camera setting to "Allow", and refresh the page. Your connection must be served over a secure HTTPS protocol for the camera API to function.
